About

Guess the Gaadi is a small trivia game about cars sold in India, built to celebrate the quirky, overlooked details that make each model memorable — not just the obvious facts everyone already knows.

The question pool covers everyday hatchbacks and SUVs, classic cars like the Ambassador and Padmini, and a few luxury halo models, each with facts written and checked by hand rather than generated automatically.

This is an independent fan project and isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any car manufacturer named in the game.
