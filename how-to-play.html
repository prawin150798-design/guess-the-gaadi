How to Play

Each round shows a clue about a real car currently or once sold in India — something about it that most people overlook.

  • Start: you get an opening clue, the car's body type and era, and a blank number plate showing how many letters the model name has.
  • Guess: pick a brand, then a model, from the two dropdowns. The brand list only shows a handful of realistic rivals, not the whole market.
  • Wrong guess: unlocks a new hint — another unique quality about the car, then its spec sheet, then its brand, then two letters on the plate.
  • Scoring: the fewer hints you use, the more points you earn. Answer streaks add a bonus on top.
  • Game length: 10 cars per game, picked at random from a much larger question pool, so no two playthroughs are quite the same.

At the end you get a rank, from Learner's Licence up to Gaadi Guru, plus a shareable result you can send to a friend.
